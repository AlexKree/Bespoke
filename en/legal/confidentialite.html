Protecting your data is central to the relationship of trust we build with our clients and partners. This policy explains what data we process, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and how to exercise your rights, in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act.

1. Data controller

Your data is processed by Bespoke (trading as The Bespoke Car), SIRET 953 834 926 00016, VAT FR37953834926, registered office at 1530 chemin de Peyniblou, 06560 Sophia-Antipolis, France.

For any question about your data: contact@thebespokecar.com.

2. Data we process

We only collect the data needed for our services, directly from you or when you use the website:

  • Enquiries and contact: first and last name, email, phone number, company, country and the content of your request (vehicle search, import, inspection, valuation…).
  • Client account: login details (passwords are stored in irreversibly encrypted form), reservations, messages exchanged with our team, stock alerts and your file history.
  • Transactions: vehicles reserved or purchased, amounts, deposits, invoices and the documents required for the sale, import or registration. Card details are entered directly with our payment provider: we never have access to them.
  • Photos and documents you send us (inspection pre-report, file documents).
  • Browsing data: anonymous traffic statistics, listing view counters and the technical data required for the website to work (IP address, browser).

We do not process any special category data within the meaning of Article 9 GDPR, and our services are not intended for minors.

4. Professional partner area

The area reserved for professionals (trade.thebespokecar.com/pro) is available by invitation once your application has been approved. There we process the details of your company and staff, your orders, payments, documents, messages and claims.

Session log. To secure your account and manage our business relationship, we record the date, time and duration of each session, the IP address and the approximate location derived from it (city, country), the device and browser type, and the pages viewed in the area, including vehicle pages. The location is computed on our own servers and is not sent to any third party. This log is only available to our team and is kept for 13 months.

Questions asked in the area may receive a first answer from an automated assistant (see section 9); our team is notified by email or messaging and takes over whenever needed.

8. Cookies and analytics

We use no advertising cookies and no third-party trackers. The website only sets cookies that are strictly necessary for it to work (keeping you signed in, security), which do not require consent. Display preferences (such as language) may be stored in your browser.

Traffic is measured with Plausible, a European tool that sets no cookies, collects no personal data and cannot follow you across websites.

9. Artificial intelligence tools

Some services rely on artificial intelligence models provided by Anthropic: the vehicle sourcing concierge, the photo-based inspection pre-report, the partner area assistant and the reading of documents in our files. Only the information needed for the task is sent, and our provider is contractually bound not to use it to train its models. Automated answers are indicative: no decision producing legal effects concerning you is based solely on automated processing, and a member of our team is always available for any question.

10. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures: encrypted connections (HTTPS), irreversibly encrypted passwords, two-factor authentication for the administration of the partner area, access restricted to authorised staff, regular backups and access logging. If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we notify the French data protection authority (CNIL) and, where required, the people concerned, within the legal deadlines.

11. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection (in particular to processing based on our legitimate interest), the right to withdraw your consent at any time, and the right to give instructions about what happens to your data after your death.

To exercise them, write to contact@thebespokecar.com or to the postal address above. We reply within one month; proof of identity may be requested if there is reasonable doubt. Some data must nonetheless be kept to meet our legal obligations.

If, after contacting us, you believe your rights have not been respected, you may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France — www.cnil.fr) or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

12. Changes

This policy may change to reflect our services or the regulations. The update date is shown at the top of the page; in the event of a significant change, we will let you know by email or in your account.
